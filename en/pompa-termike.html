Fujitsu · Haier

Heat pumps

They heat the radiators, the floor and your bathroom water with electricity, with no oil and no wood. For every 1 kWh of electricity they give 3 to 5 kWh of heat. Choose monobloc or split.

Choose the type Monobloc Split Controls and accessories
01

Takes heat from the air

The outdoor unit draws heat from the air, even when it is cold outside.

02

Moves it into water

The hot water goes to the radiators, the underfloor heating or the fan coils.

03

Heats your bathroom water too

With a built-in or separate tank, you have hot water all day.
