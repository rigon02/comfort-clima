Haier · Indoor unit · Duct

Slim duct 40 Pa

Only 198 mm high and very quiet, for bedrooms and hotel rooms. Horizontal or vertical installation.

Indoor unit · Duct

R32 / R410A

The 40 Pa slim duct is Haier's MRV indoor unit for bedrooms, hotel rooms and quiet spaces. It is only 198 mm high, installs horizontally or vertically, and works with R32 or in R410A systems.

Eight sizes from 1.3 to 7.1 kW cooling. The static pressure is selectable at 0 or 40 Pa, the condensate drain pump comes as standard, and there is a pre-cut fresh air inlet. Each size also comes in an external valve version (models ...C).

Features

  • Only 198 mm

    Very slim, it fits even into low ceilings.

  • Horizontal or vertical installation

    The unit can also be installed upright.

  • 0 or 40 Pa static pressure

    The pressure is chosen to suit the duct length.

  • Condensate drain pump

    Included as standard.

  • Bottom or rear air return

    The air intake changes by moving the panel.

  • Fresh air

    A pre-cut fresh air inlet.

  • 7 fan speeds

    DC inverter fan motor, with 7 speeds from the wired controller.

  • External valve (optional)

    The ...C version has the electronic valve outside the unit, for very quiet spaces.
