Haier · Ceiling

Ceiling-floor

Mounts under the ceiling or low on the wall, in sizes up to 16 kW, with a fresh air inlet.

Ceiling

AC25 to 160S2S

Ceiling-floor is Haier's unit that mounts under the ceiling or low on the wall, for offices and shops. It is slim, with an LED display and five fan speeds.

Eight sizes from 2.5 to 16 kW. Flow+ carries the air to every corner of the room, the fresh air inlet gives continuous ventilation, and it has hOn Wi-Fi and UVC sterilisation. For some sizes Haier also lists other outdoor units, three-phase among them; you will find them in the spec sheet.

Features

  • Flow+

    The air spreads evenly to every corner of the room. On the ceiling the unit can be mounted up to 4.2 m high.

  • Fresh air

    The prepared fresh air inlet brings in up to 20% of the unit's rated airflow.

  • 5 fan speeds

    Very high, high, medium, low and very low, for more choice in airflow.

  • Silence 33 dB(A)

    The fan, air ducts and motor are designed for a low noise level.

  • On/Off card

    With hotel room key cards, the air conditioner switches on or off by itself when the guest comes in or leaves.

  • UVC sterilisation

    The UVC module helps keep the indoor air clean and healthy.

  • hOn Wi-Fi

    With the hOn app you control the air conditioner from your phone or tablet, also with voice assistants.
