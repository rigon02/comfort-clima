About Haier

From Qingdao, since 1984.

Haier is a Chinese company based in Qingdao and one of the largest home appliance makers in the world. In air conditioning it offers split units with phone control, air-to-water heat pumps with natural R290 refrigerant, modular chillers and MRV VRF systems for buildings.

1984
Founded in Qingdao, China.
1991
Takes over the Qingdao air conditioner factory and starts in air conditioning.
30+
Years of air conditioning systems in Europe.
16
Air conditioner factories, eight of them outside China.
