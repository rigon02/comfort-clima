An air-to-water heat pump takes heat from the outdoor air and transfers it into the water of your heating system: underfloor heating, radiators or fan coils. The same unit can also heat your domestic hot water through a tank. The models we supply are rated up to A+++ for heating.

Yes. Fujitsu Comfort heat pumps keep heating down to -20°C, and Fujitsu High Power and Haier R290 models down to -25°C. For Kosovo winters, what matters is sizing the capacity to the building's heat loss, which we do when preparing the quotation.

In a monobloc heat pump the whole refrigerant circuit is in the outdoor unit and only water pipes come inside, which makes installation simpler. In a split heat pump the refrigerant flows between the outdoor and indoor units. The choice depends on the building, the distance between the units and your hot water needs.

Often, yes. It depends on the water temperature your radiators need and the heat pump's capacity at design conditions. Haier R290 heat pumps reach water temperatures up to 80°C, so they suit many radiator systems. Send us your system details and we'll assess it.

Yes, and it's the most efficient combination. Underfloor heating runs on water at around 35°C, while radiators usually need hotter water, so the heat pump uses less energy when the water circulates through the floor pipes. We supply both the heat pump and the underfloor heating pipes and components. In summer, with suitable models, the same floor can also help with cooling.

An air conditioner heats and cools the air in the room where it's installed and is a quick solution for one or a few rooms. An air-to-water heat pump heats water for the whole house, through underfloor heating, radiators or fan coils, and heats your domestic hot water at the same time. For heating a whole home, a heat pump is usually the more complete solution.

A fan coil, also called a fan convector, is an indoor unit with a fan that takes hot or chilled water from a heat pump or chiller and distributes it into the room. The same fan coil heats in winter and cools in summer. We supply Aertesi fan coils for homes, offices and hotels.

Yes. A reversible heat pump, or a chiller with fan coils, heats in winter and cools in summer. For apartments and offices, inverter air conditioners and VRF systems also provide heating and cooling in the same unit.

For one or two rooms, a split air conditioner is enough. For apartments with several rooms, a multi-split system connects several indoor units to one outdoor unit. For hotels, offices and commercial buildings, we recommend VRF systems or a chiller with fan coils.

Well-insulated buildings trap moisture, CO₂ and odours inside. A heat recovery unit continuously brings in fresh air and extracts stale air while keeping the heat: Dogu residential units recover up to 94% of the heat, according to the manufacturer.

A single-room wall unit, such as the O.ERRE GHOST-U, installs without ducts and suits individual rooms or renovations. A ducted system, such as the Dogu HOME, ventilates the whole home from one central unit and is the better choice for new builds.
