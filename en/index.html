02 · Winter

Heat your home with less electricity.

A heat pump does not make heat. It takes it from the air outside and brings it in. For every 1 kWh of electricity, it gives up to 4 kWh of heating.

  • Models that heat even at -15°C
  • Many models switch on from your phone, before you get home
  • When subsidies open, we tell you which models qualify

Windows closed. Fresh air.

In winter the air outside is often heavy. Ventilation with heat recovery brings in filtered air and keeps the heat you have paid for.
