Aertesi · Wall or floor

Aertesi ZEFIRO

Aertesi's full fan coil range, from small rooms to halls. Performance certified by Eurovent.

Wall or floor

AC or EC motor · 2 or 4 pipes

ZEFIRO is Aertesi's most flexible fan coil range, from small rooms to halls: cased or uncased, vertical or horizontal, low profile (LOW Zefiro) or recessed.

The EC brushless motor with continuous 0–10 V control adjusts output and noise to demand, with up to 67% lower consumption than traditional AC motors; there is also an AC motor version up to 4 pipes. Cooling runs from 0.6 to 10.7 kW and heating from 0.6 to 11.7 kW. Each unit is designed, assembled and tested in Aertesi's factories in Italy.

Features

  • Many versions

    Cased VA, HA, VB, HB, VL, HL; uncased VC, VD, VE, HC, HD, VF; low profile LOW Zefiro.

  • Simple installation

    Interlocking side panels make assembly quick and precise.

  • EC motor

    Continuous 0–10 V control, low noise and up to 67% lower consumption.

  • Air quality

    It can be combined with SANI FAN, BIOXAIR and FILTRASAN for sanitising and filtering.

  • Accessories

    Counter frames, panels, plenums, supervision accessories and a horizontal reversing kit (HDP).

Technical data

Data from the Aertesi ZEFIRO catalogue, Eurovent certified. In each cell: high / medium / low fan speed. Cooling with air at 27°C dry bulb (19°C wet bulb), heating with air at 20°C. With 4 pipes, heating comes from the extra 1-row B1 coil. Room sound pressure is 9 dB(A) below sound power (100 m³ room, 0.5 s reverberation time). VB and HB: add 100 mm for the plinths.
