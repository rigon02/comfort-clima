MRV 5-RC

Heat recovery: cools and heats at the same time, in different zones. Up to 22 HP from one module.

Outdoor unit · Heat recovery

2 or 3 pipes

MRV 5-RC is Haier's heat recovery VRF outdoor unit: it cools some zones and heats others at the same time, with full DC inverter. Haier offers it with 2 or 3 pipes; the spec sheet below is for the 3-pipe version.

One module runs from 8 to 22 HP (22.4 to 60 kW); up to 4 modules combine for up to 88 HP (240 kW), with up to 64 indoor units and up to 1,000 m of piping in total. The table shows the single modules; you will find the combinations in Haier's catalogue.

Features

  • Cooling and heating at once

    Heat recovery moves heat from the zones being cooled to the zones being heated.

  • From 8 to 88 HP

    One module up to 22 HP, four modules up to 88 HP.

  • Piping up to 1,000 m

    Long pipe runs, for large buildings and more freedom in installation.

  • 110 Pa static pressure

    The fan works with up to 110 Pa static pressure, even with a discharge duct.

  • Flexible installation

    Thanks to its design, the unit can be installed in many ways and in different spaces.

  • Built-in Modbus

    The MODBUS RTU protocol is inside the unit, which connects directly to a BMS with no extra gateway.

  • Smart Link (optional)

    A wireless link between indoor and outdoor units, in place of the communication cable.
