How is air cleaning chosen for a kitchen?

By the type of cooking, because the amount of grease and odour varies widely. For a light load, such as a café, an exhaust fan rated for continuous high-temperature operation is enough. For medium to very high loads, an electrostatic filter with activated carbon is added (KITCHEN). For smoke from charcoal grills and wood-fired ovens, the air is washed with water (SMOKE).
