Fujitsu

Multi-split

Several indoor units on a single outdoor unit. Fewer units on the facade, and every room at its own temperature.

01

One outdoor unit

Just one unit on the facade or the balcony, instead of several.

02

Up to 5 rooms

Each room gets its own indoor unit: wall-mounted, floor, cassette or ducted.

03

A temperature for each room

Each room is switched on, off and adjusted on its own, with a remote or from your phone.
