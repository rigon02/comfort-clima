Ceiling KRTA

Hangs under the ceiling, with no need for a false ceiling. For shops, restaurants and offices.

Ceiling

ABEG18 to 54KRTA

Fujitsu's KRTA ceiling unit hangs under the ceiling, with no need for a false ceiling. It suits shops, restaurants and offices.

Sizes from 5.2 to 13.4 kW, with single-phase 230 V outdoor units, or three-phase 400 V from 9.5 kW up. The pipes and the condensate drain connect to the right, left, back or downwards.

Features

  • Light, elegant design

    A smooth, softly rounded surface that feels comfortable in the room.

  • Flexible installation

    The drain hose and the pipes fit inside the casing and connect to the right, left, at an angle or downwards.

  • Easy installation

    A newly designed mounting kit makes hanging the unit under the ceiling simple.
