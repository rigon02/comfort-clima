Haier · Outdoor unit

Haier MRV 5-H

Haier MRV 5-H. Continuous heating, even during defrost. Up to 26 HP from one module, up to 104 HP with four.

Outdoor unit

Up to 4 modules

MRV 5-H is Haier's VRF outdoor unit that keeps heating even while it defrosts: in some defrost modes the four-way valve does not reverse, so the indoor temperature does not drop.

One module runs from 8 to 26 HP (25.2 to 73.5 kW); up to 4 modules combine for up to 104 HP (294 kW), with up to 64 indoor units and up to 1,000 m of piping in total. The 8 to 16 HP modules have a single fan and a small footprint. The table shows the single modules; you will find the combinations in Haier's catalogue.

Features

  • Continuous heating

    Direct defrosting reduces indoor temperature swings and heating carries on during defrost.

  • From 8 to 104 HP

    One module up to 26 HP, four modules up to 104 HP.

  • Piping up to 1,000 m

    Long pipe runs, for large buildings and more freedom in installation.

  • 0.5°C control

    Precise temperature, for fresh and comfortable air.

  • 110 Pa static pressure

    The fan works with up to 110 Pa static pressure, even with a discharge duct.

  • Built-in Modbus

    The MODBUS RTU protocol is inside the unit, which connects directly to a BMS with no extra gateway.

  • Smart Link (optional)

    A ZigBee wireless link between indoor and outdoor units, in place of the communication cable. Each unit repeats the signal, so it reaches even the farthest unit.

  • Wi-Fi control

    The unit is easy to control over Wi-Fi.
