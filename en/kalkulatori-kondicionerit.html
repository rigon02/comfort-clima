01

How the air conditioner class is chosen

The recommendation is based on the room area: up to 25 m² 9,000 BTU models, 25 to 40 m² 12,000 BTU, 40 to 60 m² 14,000 BTU, 60 to 70 m² 18,000 BTU and 70 to 80 m² 24,000 BTU.

02

The indoor unit type

The result shows only the type of indoor unit you choose: wall-mounted, floor, cassette, ducted or ceiling. We take the room’s other conditions into account in the quotation.
