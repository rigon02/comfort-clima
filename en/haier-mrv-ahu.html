Haier · AHU kit

MRV AHU

Connects air handling units to the VRF, with no cooling towers, large water pipes or pumps.

AHU kit

R410A

MRV AHU is Haier's kit that connects third-party air handling units (AHU) to the VRF, with direct expansion. Compared with a water system, no cooling towers, large water pipes or pumps are needed.

One box covers 3.5 to 73 kW, and up to 3 boxes connect in parallel for larger capacity. It works with the MRV 5 and MRV S II (4 to 12 HP) outdoor units, with R410A, and AHUs and MRV indoor units can share the same system.

Features

  • Third-party AHUs

    It controls third-party air handling units.

  • From 3.5 to 73 kW

    With a single valve, up to 3 boxes in parallel.

  • FUJIKOKI valve

    The expansion valve is made by FUJIKOKI, Japan.

  • Same boards as MRV

    The same electronic boards as the MRV indoor units, for simple management and maintenance.

  • Control signals

    0–10 V DDC input from the third-party controller, inputs for heat / cool and 3 fan speeds, and a defrost status output.

  • Individual or central control

    With Haier's control solutions.
