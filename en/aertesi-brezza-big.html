Aertesi BREZZA BIG

Aertesi BREZZA BIG. A larger cassette for halls, shops and open-plan offices with 900 × 900 tiled ceilings.

Cassette

900 × 900

BREZZA BIG is Aertesi's large hydronic cassette for 900 × 900 mm tiled ceilings, for hotels, open-plan offices, shops and restaurants. The air flows along the ceiling and comes down gently along the walls (the Coanda effect), with no unpleasant draughts.

The metal panel does not yellow and can be painted to match the ceiling. It comes with an AC or EC motor, for 2 or 4-pipe systems. The BREZZA BIG H version runs without a condensate pump, more hygienic and quieter, and there is also a MECO version with a metal cover for exposed installation.

Features

  • Coanda effect

    Air in 4 directions along the ceiling, with no direct jets.

  • Two panels

    MPK-C with fixed fins; MPK-D with movable deflectors, for vertical flow too.

  • BREZZA BIG H

    Cleaning or filtration function according to the model configuration. Operation and maintenance are described in the relevant documentation.

  • EC motor

    Up to 52% lower consumption; an AC version is also available.

  • Full access

    Every part is reached from the front panel, which opens safely from below.

  • Certified parts

    EUROVENT certified components and an anti-vibration structure.

Technical data

Data from the Aertesi BREZZA BIG catalogue, Eurovent certified. In each cell: high / medium / low fan speed. Cooling with air at 27°C dry bulb (19°C wet bulb), heating with air at 20°C. Room sound pressure: 100 m³ room, 0.5 s reverberation time.
