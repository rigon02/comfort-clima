Dogu · Grilles and louvres

Acoustic louvre

An acoustic louvre with rock wool filled blades, in three models: DPS-150, DPS-300 and DPS-600.

Grilles and louvres

DPS

DPS is Dogu's acoustic louvre, for air intake and discharge where noise must be reduced. The blades are filled with 50 kg/m³ rock wool, and the surfaces facing the airflow are covered with glass fibre against abrasion.

The casing and blades are galvanised sheet, or stainless steel on request. The blade shape also keeps rain out, and it comes with a bird screen. Three models, DPS-150, DPS-300 and DPS-600, for different needs.
