Aertesi · Cassette

Aertesi BREZZA

Aertesi BREZZA is a cassette fan coil for integration into a modular ceiling. Its air distribution uses the Coanda effect, with size and fan speed selected for the room requirements.

Cassette

600 × 600

BREZZA is Aertesi's hydronic cassette for 600 × 600 mm tiled ceilings, for offices, shops and hotels. The 0.8 mm painted steel panel sits flush with the ceiling.

The special diffusion frame sends the air along the ceiling and then down along the walls (the Coanda effect), with no strong draughts on people and an even temperature. It comes with an AC or EC motor, for 2 or 4-pipe systems.

Cooling capacity
2.3 to 5.4 kW
Heating capacity

Airflow

AC or EC motor · 2 or 4 pipes

Features

  • Coanda effect

    The air spreads 4 ways along the ceiling, with no direct jets.

  • Two panels

    MPK-C with fixed horizontal fins; MPK-D with manually adjustable fins, vertical too, for ceilings over 3 m.

  • EC motor

    Up to 56% lower consumption; an AC version is also available.

  • Installation and maintenance

    The valve kit and electrical board sit on the same side, and every part is reached from the front panel.

  • Solid structure

    Thick steel with integrated brackets, which cuts vibration and noise; the panel does not yellow over time.

Technical data

Data from the Aertesi BREZZA catalogue, Eurovent certified. In each cell: high / medium / low fan speed. Cooling with air at 27°C dry bulb (19°C wet bulb), heating with air at 20°C. Room sound pressure is 9 dB(A) below sound power (100 m³ room, 0.5 s reverberation time).
