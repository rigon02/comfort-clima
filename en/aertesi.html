About Aertesi

From Italy, for indoor air.

Aertesi is an Italian company based in Conselve, near Padua, that designs and builds air conditioning and air treatment equipment. The range includes fan coils, air handling units, dehumidification systems, heat pumps and hybrid systems, for Italy and for foreign markets.
