Aertesi AER SLIM EVO

Aertesi AER SLIM EVO is a slim-profile fan coil unit with an EC motor. The range includes exposed and concealed installation options, depending on the VB, HB or VD configuration and available space.

Wall or floor

VB · HB · VD versions

AER SLIM EVO is Aertesi's slim fan coil for homes and commercial spaces: quiet, efficient and at home in any interior. It comes vertical or horizontal, cased (VB and HB) or recessed into the wall (VD, with panel and counter frame).

It heats, cools and dehumidifies, for 2-pipe systems and low temperature generators. The patented EC brushless motor control holds the set point with very little power, with comfort close to a radiant panel. Cooling reaches 3.28 kW and heating 3.51 kW on the larger sizes.

Features

  • Very slim

    A small footprint with a neutral look.

  • Almost radiant

    In heating the EC motor holds the set point with very little power and noise.

  • Four seasons

    It heats, cools and dehumidifies.

  • Quiet operation

    Down to 28 dB(A) at low speed, even for bedrooms.

  • BLDC inverter motor

    Up to 72% lower consumption than traditional AC motors.

  • No plastic parts

    A structure that does not deteriorate over time and cuts maintenance.

  • Control

    Advanced electronics, adaptive functions, home automation or Modbus.
