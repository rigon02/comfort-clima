Haier

Chillers

Modular chillers and commercial heat pumps for hotels, offices, hospitals and residential buildings. Chilled water in summer and hot water in winter, from modules that grow with the project.

01

Prepares the water

The chiller or commercial heat pump cools or heats the water for the whole building.

02

Carries it through the building

Through water pipes, not refrigerant lines, to every floor and every room.

03

Fan coils and AHUs in the rooms

Fan coils set the room temperature, and air handling units bring in fresh air.
