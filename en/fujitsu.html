About Fujitsu

A Japanese company, since 1936.

Fujitsu General makes air conditioners and heat pumps for homes, businesses and buildings. The range goes from inverter units for a single room to Airstage VRF systems for whole buildings, and air-to-water heat pumps for heating and hot water.

1936
Founded in Japan. Headquarters in Kawasaki.
1977
The first sales offices for European markets open.
2008
A 60 metre tower for testing VRF systems opens at the Kawasaki headquarters.
2022
Production of air-to-water heat pumps starts inside Europe.
