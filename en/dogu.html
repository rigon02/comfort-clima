About Dogu

From Izmir, since 1999.

Dogu (DOGU HVAC) is a Turkish manufacturer with its headquarters and factories in Izmir. From the start its main product has been air handling units; today the range also includes heat recovery units, rooftop units, kitchen ventilation, air purifiers, grilles and diffusers.

1999
Founded in Izmir, Turkey.
