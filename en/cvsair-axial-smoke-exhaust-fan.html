Cvsair · Smoke extraction

Cvsair axial smoke exhaust fan F300 / F400

Cvsair axial smoke exhaust fan F300 / F400. Extracts polluted air and smoke from car parks, industrial buildings, airports and tunnels. Aluminium blades with adjustable pitch.

Smoke extraction

F300 · F400

Cvsair's axial smoke exhaust fans take out the polluted air and smoke brought by jet fans or ducts, from car parks, industrial buildings, airports and tunnels. They come in classes F300 and F400 to EN 12101-3, with the same diameters and motors.

The casing is hot-dip galvanised, the aluminium blades have an aerofoil section and adjustable pitch, and the three-phase IE2 motors work with frequency inverters. They install outdoors or indoors, vertically or horizontally. Eleven diameters, from Ø400 to Ø1250 mm.
