Calculator

Which system suits your home?

Answer a few simple questions about your home and see which systems fit, with a rough capacity and the products of ours worth a look. It is a preliminary estimate, not a design.

    Before you start

    Five short steps, then your estimate.

    About 3 minutes. Where you do not know an answer, pick “Not sure”: the estimate allows for it.

    • Location and climate
    • Your home and its area
    • The building: age, insulation and windows
    • The heating and cooling you have today
    • What matters most to you

    You do not need to give a name, phone or email to see the estimate. Your answers are not sent anywhere: they stay on this page unless you decide to write to us.

    01

    Building and area

    We start from the area you want to heat or cool. For heating we use rule-of-thumb figures from about 35 to 55 W/m² for new, well insulated homes, up to about 105 to 140 W/m² for old homes without insulation.

    02

    Sun and climate

    For cooling we start from about 60 to 125 W/m² for one room, a little less for a whole home, and adjust for sun, glass, high ceilings, the climate and the number of people. Every “Not sure” widens the range.

    03

    System and products

    We choose the kind of system from what you have (radiators, underfloor, ducts) and what you want. The products are from our range. Only a room-by-room calculation gives the final size.

    Several rooms or a whole building?

    For several rooms see multi-split, for buildings the VRF systems or chillers with fan coils. Send us the floor plan and we will help you choose.
