Aertesi SOFFIO / SOFFIO HP

Aertesi SOFFIO / SOFFIO HP. Hydronic ducted units above a false ceiling. SOFFIO up to 70 Pa, SOFFIO HP with high static pressure up to 150 Pa for long ducts.

Ducted

SOFFIO 21 · 31 · 38 · 41 · 81 · SOFFIO HP

SOFFIO and SOFFIO HP are Aertesi's hydronic ducted units, installed above a false ceiling, for offices, shops and professional spaces. SOFFIO delivers 70 Pa at nominal airflow; SOFFIO HP delivers 150 Pa, for long or branched duct networks.

The casing is 1 to 1.5 mm galvanised steel with acoustic insulation, the coils are copper/aluminium (PN8), and the filter comes out from below or the side without tools. The motor is AC with 3 speeds or EC with continuous control. SOFFIO also comes with an R410A direct expansion coil.

Features

  • 70 or 150 Pa

    SOFFIO up to 70 Pa, SOFFIO HP 150 Pa at nominal airflow.

  • Quiet running

    Thick galvanised steel and self-extinguishing acoustic insulation; on HP 10 mm polyethylene, class B-s2d0 to EN 13501-1.

  • Easy maintenance

    The filter comes out without tools, and the ducts stay connected for routine maintenance.

  • Copper/aluminium coils

    With corrugated fins and accessible air vents.

  • EC motor

    Up to 20% savings on SOFFIO and up to 35% on SOFFIO HP.

  • BMS

    Up to 24 units master/slave, supervision of up to 240 (SOFFIO) or 250 (HP) units with TOP3, SP3 and Modbus.

  • Versions

    Single (SP) or double (DP) panel; SOFFIO also horizontal or vertical (HC, VC, VD).

Technical data

Data from the Aertesi SOFFIO and SOFFIO HP catalogues. In each cell: high / medium / low fan speed. Cooling with air at 27°C dry bulb (19°C wet bulb), heating with air at 20°C. SOFFIO values are Eurovent certified, except size 81. With 4 pipes, heating comes from the extra 1-row coil.
