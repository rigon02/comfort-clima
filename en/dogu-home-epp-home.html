Dogu · For homes

HOME / EPP HOME

Fresh air all year, with a polypropylene counterflow exchanger and automatic defrost.

For homes

Residential heat recovery unit

HOME and EPP HOME are Dogu's heat recovery units for homes that need fresh air all year round. Heat recovery greatly reduces the heating and cooling load of the house.

The polypropylene counterflow exchanger reaches up to 94% efficiency, with automatic defrost and 19 mm acoustic insulation. HOME has a galvanised steel casing; EPP HOME (sizes 35 and 50) is made of expanded polypropylene, light and 100% recyclable. Five sizes, from about 240 to 640 m³/h.

Features

  • Up to 94% recovery

    A polypropylene counterflow exchanger.

  • Automatic defrost

    Frost protection for all operating conditions.

  • G4 and F7 filters

    A G4 filter on the supply side, F7 as an option, with continuous monitoring of filter dirt.

  • EC plug fans

    High efficiency, with the 3-step speed switch supplied with the unit.

  • Bypass

    Free cooling and frost protection for the exchanger.

  • EPP HOME

    Density 45 expanded polypropylene: light, resistant to impact, moisture and chemicals, with good thermal and acoustic insulation.

  • Options

    Electric heater, DX or water coil, temperature, CO₂ and pressure sensors, wireless too.

  • ErP 2018

    Compliant with the ErP 2018 regulation.
