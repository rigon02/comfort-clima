Flexis Plus

Standard Wi-Fi and UVC light sterilisation. Very quiet, for every room in the house.

Wall-mounted

AS25/35/42/50/71S2SF1FA

Flexis Plus is Haier's wall unit with hOn Wi-Fi as standard, UVC light sterilisation and very quiet operation, in white or matt black (models -MW3 and -MB3).

Five sizes: 2.5, 3.5, 4.2, 5.0 and 7.1 kW, with a class up to A+++ in cooling and A++ in heating. The Eco sensor and I Feel adapt the unit to people in the room. For cold areas there are also Nordic outdoor units with a heating cable, in 2.5, 3.5 and 5.0 kW.

Features

  • UVC sterilisation

    The UVC module helps keep the indoor air clean and healthy.

  • 56°C Steri-Clean

    The indoor heat exchanger is heated to 56°C for 30 minutes to kill bacteria and viruses, then cooled down at once.

  • Eco sensor

    The sensor detects people up to 8 m away across 120°, sends the air towards them or away from them, and switches to energy saving within 20 minutes when the room is empty.

  • Silence

    The fan, air ducts and motor are designed for a low noise level.

  • I Feel

    The remote control measures the temperature where you are in the room, and the unit works to that reading.

  • 10°C Vacation mode

    It switches on when the temperature drops below 10°C, to protect pipes in winter in empty holiday homes, garages and basements.

  • Heating cable (Nordic)

    The Nordic version of the outdoor unit has a heating cable for cold winters.

  • hOn Wi-Fi

    With the hOn app you control the air conditioner from your phone or tablet, also with voice assistants.
