01

Heat loss

The area is multiplied by the loss per m² for the insulation and the design temperature: for example 55 W/m² for medium insulation at −15°C. Between temperatures the value is worked out in a straight line.

02

Radiators and hot water

With radiators we allow 15% more power, because they run on hotter water. The domestic hot water tank is sized at about 45 L per person, and the buffer tank by the heat pump's capacity.

03

Models from our range

We show the models that cover the need without going over double, with the tanks that suit them. The final size comes from a room-by-room calculation.

Several rooms or a whole building?

For several rooms see multi-split, for buildings the VRF systems or chillers with fan coils. Send us the floor plan and we will help you choose.
